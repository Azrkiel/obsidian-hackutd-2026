## 8. Decisions, rationale, invariants
- Decision runs at the **last corner's exit**, not the lap line (cue must play on the main straight; lap mode caps model time at 8 s). So `packet.lap.last_lap_time_s` is the previous lap.
- Only one goal cued per lap (plan rule "one change at a time"); plan ≤ 3 goals; goal 1 = top corner by time lost among corners with a metric over tolerance.
- Attempt ids are `L<lap>-<corner_id>`; corner names are spoken, keep them short ("Lesmo 2" digits are allowed via `corner_numbers`).
- In whole-lap AC mode, position = normalized position × `length_m` (5793) so corners stay fractions of the lap (`ACSharedMemory(fixed_length=True)`).
- Start gate = F1 brake onset − 250 m, clamped to keep ≥ 80 m talk gap after the previous corner; board zero = 15 m before the first apex. Do not shrink the Lesmo 2 start gate (only 67 m before the 787B's estimated braking).
- Drill mode stays single-corner (`--corner`); `--track` forces lap mode.
- `.claude/` and `CLAUDE.md` are gitignored on purpose (user: "no claude.md"); never commit them.

## 9. Assumptions made
- AC Monza's lap line = F1 timing line (positions measured from the F1 line).
- AC Monza length = 5793 m and corners are at the same lap fractions as the real circuit.
- Track Titan's "metres before the apex" is measured to the first apex marker (MultiViewer corner position).
- Board zero ≈ 15 m before the first apex (F1 then brakes at the T1 100 board). Real AC board positions unknown.
- Sim apex speeds/boards = Track Titan 787B numbers; the sim reference is only valid for the sim.
